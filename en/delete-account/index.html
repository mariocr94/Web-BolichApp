Delete your BolichApp account and data

BolichApp is an app by TODO: developer name (same as on Google Play). This page explains how to delete your account and data, what is deleted and what is kept.

From the app (recommended)

  1. Open BolichApp.
  2. Go to Settings → Your data.
  3. Tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm with Delete everything.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you want to keep your games, export a backup from the same screen first. Without a connection, the app offers to delete only what is on the phone; you can repeat the process online to also delete your user on the server.

What is deleted

  • On your phone: your profile and aliases, balls, centers, sessions, games and rolls, kept scoreboard photos, and the app’s temporary backup files.
  • On our server: your user (anonymous or Google) and any quota setting linked to it.
  • If you signed in with Google: your email, your name and all your synced data. TODO: confirm when sync is implemented.

What is kept

  • Photo usage log, without an identifier. For each photo read we keep the date, outcome, model, tokens, estimated cost, image size and error code, but it is detached from your user when the user is deleted, so it can no longer be linked to anyone. It never contained images, names or aliases. We use it for cost accounting and the service’s daily limit. TODO: maximum retention period (e.g. 24 months).
  • Server technical logs (no images or names), which are deleted automatically. TODO: period per the Supabase plan.
  • Backups you exported and shared (for example, on WhatsApp or Google Drive): these are out of our reach; delete them from that service.

We never store scoreboard photos on the server, so there are no photos to delete there.

If you no longer have the app

If you signed in with Google, email us from that account’s address at marioacr94@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my BolichApp account”. We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and delete your account and synced data within 20 business days; we will let you know when it’s done. TODO: confirm periods.

If you never signed in with Google, your data was only on your phone: Android deletes it when you uninstall the app. If you used “From photo”, the server only keeps an anonymous identifier and its usage log, with no email or name, which we cannot link to you.
