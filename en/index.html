BolichApp

Log your bowling games in seconds, understand your numbers and find out what to practice.

Coming soon to Google Play for Android.

What you can do

  • Fast entry

    Tap the pins left standing and you’re done: the app scores with the official rules and flags splits for you. There’s also a keypad with X, / and numbers.

  • From photo

    Take a photo of the bowling center scoreboard and the app reads your game. You always review it before saving. Requires a Google account.

  • In-depth stats

    Average and consistency, strike and spare rates, splits, most common leaves, the tenth frame, and results by ball and by bowling center.

  • What to practice

    Spots patterns in your game, like dropping off in your last game or the pins that cost you most, only when there’s enough data.

Your data stays on your phone

BolichApp works without an account and offline: your games, balls and sessions are stored only on your phone. If you want, sign in with Google to sync your data across phones and read scoreboards from a photo.

No ads, no third-party analytics and no cookies. Read the Privacy Policy.
